Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This page explains what cookies and similar storage technologies our website currently uses, and why. We keep this list to what the site actually needs — we don't add tracking or advertising technology just for the sake of it.

1. What we currently use

NameTypePurposeDurationConsent required?
Language preference (browser local storage) Strictly necessary / functional Remembers which language (English, French, Greek, German, Italian, Spanish) you last selected, so the site displays correctly on your next visit. Until you clear your browser storage No — this is strictly necessary for the site to function as intended and stores no personal or identifying information.
Shopping cart (browser local storage) Strictly necessary / functional Remembers the items you've added to your cart while you browse, so they aren't lost if you navigate between pages before checking out. Until you clear your browser storage, complete checkout, or empty your cart No — this is strictly necessary for the ordering functionality to work and stores no personal or identifying information (only item names, quantities, and prices).
Last order reference (browser local storage) Strictly necessary / functional Remembers your most recent order number and tracking link after checkout, so you can find your order tracking page again. Until you clear your browser storage No — this is strictly necessary for the order-tracking feature and stores only an order ID and tracking token, not your personal details.

This is currently the full list of client-side storage our website uses. We do not currently use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking scripts.

2. Checkout & payment

When you check out and pay online, our payment processor, Stripe, may set additional strictly-necessary cookies purely to allow you to complete checkout securely. These are considered "strictly necessary" under EU ePrivacy rules and do not require separate consent — see our Privacy Policy for more on how Stripe handles your payment data.

3. If analytics or marketing tools are added in future

Should we choose to add optional tools such as visit analytics or marketing pixels in the future, this policy will be updated in advance, and a consent banner will ask for your permission before any non-essential cookie is set, in line with GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive.

4. Managing storage in your browser

You can clear locally stored data (such as your saved language preference) at any time through your browser's settings, typically under "Privacy" or "Site settings." Clearing this will simply mean the site defaults back to English on your next visit.
