2. What information we collect

We collect only the information needed to process your order, deliver it, and support you as a customer:

  • Identity and contact details: full name, phone number, email address.
  • Delivery information: delivery address or location, and any delivery instructions you provide.
  • Order details: items ordered, order value, order history, and any notes you add.
  • Payment confirmation data: we receive confirmation that a payment was successful and a transaction reference from our payment processor. We do not collect or store your full card number, expiry date, or CVV/CVC security code — these are entered directly with our payment processor, not on our own servers.
  • Technical information: basic, non-identifying technical data needed to display the website correctly (see our Cookie Policy for details on local storage and cookies).

4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it with third parties where necessary to complete your order:

  • Payment processor: Stripe, to securely process your card payment. The processor acts as an independent controller/processor for the payment data it handles, under its own privacy policy and PCI-DSS security obligations.
  • Delivery: your order is delivered by our own in-house delivery staff, who receive your name, phone number, and delivery address so your order can be delivered.
  • IT and hosting providers who help us run the website, under appropriate data processing agreements.
  • Public authorities, only where we are legally required to disclose information (e.g. tax authorities).

5. International data transfers

Where any of the providers above process data outside the European Economic Area, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses) before any such transfer occurs.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Order and invoicing records are kept for 5 years to comply with Greek tax and accounting law.
  • Customer account data, if applicable, is kept for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period afterwards.
  • Marketing consent and related data are kept until you withdraw consent.

7. Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data, where applicable
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us by phone at 2610 992 209. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Αρχή Προστασίας Δεδομένων Προσωπικού Χαρακτήρα), www.dpa.gr.

Card payment security: Sweet Candy never stores your full card number, expiry date, or CVV/CVC code. Card payments are handled directly by our payment processor, which is independently certified for PCI-DSS security standards.

8. Security measures

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections, access controls limiting who can view customer data, and reliance on reputable, security-certified third-party processors for payment handling.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.
