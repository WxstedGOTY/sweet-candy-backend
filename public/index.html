About

A spot for ice cream, right on the water.

Sweet Candy is located on Posidonos Street, on the seafront in Rio, Patras. A shop where ice cream, waffles, crepes, coffee and sandwiches are made with care, for anyone out for a walk by the sea.

Simple, clean, no fuss. Come in, pick what you're after, enjoy the view.
